## 2. Product direction — preserve intent

RIFT Taxi is a standalone, Python-first, **destination-first** RIFT MMO movement controller. Its ultimate success is smooth start-to-destination motion, predominantly **continuous W** with concurrent A/D steering. Ordinary guide points are pass-through and skippable when supported by traversable corridor evidence. Mandatory passages, hazards, requested hard stops and final arrival remain binding. Plan routing locally without any AI dependency at game runtime.

M1 exit requires: **fresh same-actor/zone XYZ and independently qualified body-facing yaw**, bounded physically demonstrated W/A/D action, exclusive controller ownership, **independently recovered/verified release** and a newer settled post-release observation, without sibling runtime dependencies. A test pass, unqualified native field, successful OS SendInput return or a visual camera rotation **is not M1 exit**.
